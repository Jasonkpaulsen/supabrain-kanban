Approve Codex access to Paulsen Family data

SB-410 · ADR-API-002. This page authorizes one external application to act as you, inside the family projects where you are already a member. It never widens what you can see or change.

Sign in first

Consent must come from a live Supabase login. Use your existing SupaBrain account — no new account is created.

Will return you to:

Signed in as

1. Core family Kanban (read, create, update — never delete)

  • Projects you are a member of, their members, work items, comments, labels
  • Household, holiday and travel planning items; family calendar events; school assignments

2. Sensitive child health and care records

Kai's and Jai's medications, health events, care plans (IEP/504, therapy), health providers, behavioral logs, and the read-only care audit log — for the child projects where you are a member. Medication changes will ask you to confirm each one separately and are audited. Provider portal credentials are never shown.

3. Family agents (act as a role in Codex)

Lets Codex adopt the published role profiles of the eleven approved family agents (Family PM, Family Care Manager, Care Coordinator, EF Coach, School & Activities Lead, Activities & Schedule Coordinator, School Monitor, Parent Advocate, Travel Coordinator, Travel Planner, Travel Activities Coordinator). Agents run in your session, with your permissions; nothing runs in the background, and no agent can do what you cannot.

Consent is never inferred from your email address or your relationship to the children. All three sections must be allowed for this connection to work: declining any one of them denies the whole request and leaves Codex with no access. You can revoke access at any time from the family dashboard.
